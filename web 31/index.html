Who we are

Basically just farmers

Our farm was started back in 2015. Our farmers all have great passion and permaculture skill to bring the farm to life – we practise ethical farming following the principles of permaculture and care for the earth.

More about us
Our core values

Rooted deep with our farming principles

Earth care

Care for the soil, the water and every living thing that depends on them.

People care

We look after our farmers, neighbours and guests as family.

Fair share

We share surplus and knowledge, and price fairly for everyone.

What we do

Rebuilding nature capital & sharing our ways

We’re growing mixed crops and trees in a way that completely heals the land. Through our training and our organically grown produce, we pass on the principles and methods to help a community of permaculture.
